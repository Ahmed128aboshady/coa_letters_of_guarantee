Core Capabilities

Enterprise Financial Features

Bid Bonds (Tenders)

Issue initial guarantee bonds for public and private tender submissions. Track tender opening dates, submission deadlines, and convert to Performance Bond upon award.

Performance Bonds (Final)

Guarantee project execution upon contract award. Link directly to contracting project analytic accounts, monitor execution milestones, and manage renewal cycles.

Advance Payment Guarantees

Secure client mobilization advance payments. Track guarantee value amortization as contractor progress invoices (work certificates) are submitted and approved.

Maintenance & Decennial Bonds

Cover defect liability and warranty maintenance periods post-handover. Automates final release and collateral recovery after the completion certificate is stamped.

Automated Accounting Entries

Eliminate human accounting error. The system posts proper journal entries for cash margin cover, bank commission fees, extension charges, and final release automatically.

Multi-Bank & Multi-Currency

Manage credit facility limits per issuing bank. Supports foreign currency bonds (USD, EUR, SAR, EGP) with automatic exchange rate valuation at issuance and release.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are bank margins accounted for upon letter issuance?

When a letter is marked as Issued, Odoo automatically debits your dedicated Bank Guarantee Margin account (Current Asset) and credits your main bank account. Issuance commission expenses are debited directly to financial expenses.

What happens when a guarantee is extended or renewed?

You can record an extension with the new maturity date. If the issuing bank charges an extension fee, Odoo generates the corresponding bank fee journal entry and records the full history in the chatter.

What happens upon guarantee release?

When the beneficiary releases the guarantee, clicking "Release" generates an automated reverse entry: debiting the main bank account and crediting the margin account, returning funds into your available cash.
